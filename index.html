How to use

  1. Pick a Difficulty (animation speed), a Category (word length), and how many words you want this round.
  2. Watch the animation. Use Replay or open the Alphabet if you forget a letter.
  3. Type what you saw and press Enter (or click Check Answer).
  4. Stuck? Give up & reveal, or tap How to sign in ASL to watch the real sign.
  5. Finish the round to log your accuracy, then start a new one.
  6. Want a word's meaning? Open the Results tab and tap any word in your history to look it up.

Chatting with Deaf / deaf / HOH people

✓ Do

  • Get my attention first — a wave, a light tap on the shoulder, or a flick of the lights.
  • Face me directly with your mouth and expression visible, light on your face.
  • Speak naturally and clearly; if I miss it, rephrase instead of just repeating.
  • Keep eye contact with me, not with my interpreter or companion.
  • Use text, writing, or gestures whenever speech doesn't land — whatever works.
  • Be patient, let me finish, and ask how I prefer to communicate.

✗ Don't

  • Don't cover your mouth, chew, or turn away while you're talking.
  • Don't shout or exaggerate your lips — it distorts lipreading.
  • Don't say "never mind" or "I'll tell you later" — it's dismissive.
  • Don't talk to my interpreter instead of me ("tell her…").
  • Don't assume every deaf person lipreads or uses the same language.
  • Don't start talking before you have my visual attention.

Small habits, big difference. (Press Esc to close.)
