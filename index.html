How to use

  1. Practice is untimed — explore any pack, look words up, press Enter to advance once an answer is in.
  2. Challenge is timed — pick a pack + speed, then race the clock. Each round is saved as a submission.
  3. Watch the animation. Use Replay, change Speed, or open the Alphabet if you forget a letter.
  4. Type what you saw and press Enter (or click Check Answer).
  5. For ASL-LEX words, tap See the real ASL sign to watch the actual sign.
